Version: 0.3.2
Date: 2026-10-29
  Changes:
    - Without Space Age, carbon is now made from coal in the chemical plant instead of furnaces. As a furnace recipe, furnaces fed coal from their ore belt switched to carbon whenever the ore ran out and flooded the belt with carbon.
    - The armour steel technology now requires oil processing, which unlocks the chemical plant its recipes need.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-10-28
  Changes:
    - A thrown mine is now a package of 5 land mines (recipe: 5 land mines and 1 explosives). On impact the mines scatter around the target.
    - The mine launcher remote is in the shortcut bar, like the artillery remote, and no longer crafted.
    - The mine launcher wagon has six launcher baskets that turn and fire on their own, each once every 10 seconds. Each click with the remote fires one basket. Like an artillery wagon, the baskets fire only while the train stands; before it sets off they turn home and hold the train until they are there.
  Bugfixes:
    - The mine launcher wagon no longer shows a vanilla artillery cannon floating next to it. The launcher baskets on the wagon fire the mines.
    - Roof turrets follow the wagon more closely in curves: the script now also predicts the wagon turning.
    - Roof turrets sit right on wagons turned round (driving south or east): they follow the drawn wagon, whose back looks like its front.
    - The mine launcher wagon is drawn all the way round, so it no longer looks turned when driving south or east.
    - A launcher basket's shot and muzzle flash leave from the basket that fires, no longer from one spot at the wagon.
    - In alt mode the ammo icons of the roof turrets are shown on the turrets, no longer below them.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Balancing:
    - The equipment grid of the military wagons is 6x8 like the tank's (was 16x16), so it no longer takes far more equipment than a tank.
    - Military wagons have 2000 health like the tank (was 1000).
    - Armour plates are 2x2 like an energy shield (were 4x4), with recipes a quarter of the size. A grid full of plates now protects about as well as one full of shields with a reactor, but only against its damage type and without power.
    - Plate values per plate: composite 0.5 flat / 4 %, reactive, acid and fire 5 %, tungsten plates 6 to 7 %.
    - Better quality makes a plate stronger, like a shield: +30 % per quality level.
    - Equipment in old saves that no longer fits into the smaller grid is put into the wagon's cargo, or on the ground next to it.
  Graphics:
    - New, more detailed armour plate graphics: welded seams, bolts, dents, rust, scorched tiles, acid stains.
  Bugfixes:
    - Explosive shells from the cannon wagons no longer damage your own buildings, trains and walls. Their explosions now spare your side like their impact already did.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Twin cannon wagon: two tank cannons on the roof, each turning all the way round.
    - Howitzer wagon: one long gun, range 36, minimum range 12, half the tank's rate of fire.
    - Mine launcher wagon: lays land mines up to 120 tiles away, aimed with its own remote like artillery. Minimum range 16.
    - Wagon shells are wagon-only copies of the tank shells: they fly through the own train and on to 40 tiles.
    - Armour steel (steel, copper, carbon and water in a chemical plant). Without Space Age carbon is made from coal.
    - Equipment grid on all military wagons (16x16). It takes everything a tank's grid takes, plus armour plates.
    - Seven armour plates against physical, explosion, acid and fire damage, four of them made of tungsten with Space Age. Plates fit only into military wagons. Their protection adds up to at most 80 %.
    - Wagon panel next to the wagon window: the armour in effect, target priorities and, on the rocket wagon, the minimum range (8 to 24, with range circles).

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Machine gun, flamethrower and rocket wagon in one mod. They used to be three separate mods (up to version 0.2.1).
    - Each wagon can be switched off in the startup settings.
  Info:
    - The wagons keep their names, so saves with the old mods keep their wagons. The old mods become empty bridges to this one.
